## 10-Scene Walkthrough Summary

1. **Scene 1 — Introduction (`"Who's signing in?"` Landing Portal)**: Showcases the official Bright Riders School crest, `BRIGHT RIDERS DIGITAL CANTEEN` header, and the four role-selection cards (`Student`, `Parent`, `Chef`, `Admin`).

3. **Scene 3 — Official 28-Item SEHHI Canteen Menu**: Browsing `BREAKFAST`, `HOT MEALS`, `SNACKS & DESSERT`, and `DRINKS` with food photography, vegetarian badges, and exact AED pricing.
4. **Scene 4 — Advance Meal Ordering**: Selecting child beneficiary, ordering date, break collection slot, item quantities, special kitchen instructions, and itemised AED summary.
5. **Scene 5 — Digital AED Checkout & Receipt**: Completing cashless AED checkout with duplicate-payment protection (`409`), transaction reference (`TXN-BRS-...`), Order ID (`BRS-...`), and instant QR pass issuance.
6. **Scene 6 — Student Dashboard & Cashless QR Pass**: Mobile-friendly Student Dining Pass displaying upcoming pre-ordered meal items, 4-step preparation progress, and optical white-surface QR pass.
7. **Scene 7 — Chef Kitchen Display System (KDS)**: Break-slot grouped paid order tickets, allergy/instruction banners, and live status transitions (`Preparing` → `Ready for Collection`).
8. **Scene 8 — Live Camera QR Scanning & Collection Confirmation**: WebRTC + `jsQR` camera scan verification (*"Order verified successfully. Ready for collection confirmation."*), atomic `Confirm Collection`, and automatic duplicate-scan protection (*"This order has already been collected."*).
9. **Scene 9 — Administrator Suite**: Sales KPIs, 4-role User Management (`Student`, `Parent`, `Chef`, `Admin`), Official Menu & Schedule Management, Payment & Refund controls, Cancellation Cutoff Policy, and one-click CSV exports.
10. **Scene 10 — Conclusion**: Summary of campus benefits for Parents, Students, Chefs, and School Leadership.
